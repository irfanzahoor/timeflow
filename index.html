🎯 BANT / CHAMP Qualification Framework

Budget: Is there dedicated budget for ERP improvements?
Authority: Are you speaking directly with the business owner / CTO?
Need: What specific bottleneck is causing revenue loss or friction?
Timeline: When do they need the customized workflow operational?

💬 LAER Objection Handling Framework

Listen: Let the client explain their concern without interruption.
Acknowledge: Validate their concern ("I completely understand why cost/downtime is a priority...").
Explore: Ask a clarifying question to uncover the root cause.
Respond: Position your solution, proof case study (e.g. Paymob integration), and clear SOW milestones.
